STAR · AWS cost reduction (WelTel)

Situation
Monthly AWS spend hovered near $4,150 with managed CI runners and uneven capacity planning.
Task
Cut cloud spend without sacrificing delivery velocity or multi-AZ reliability for clinical workloads.
Action
Moved CI to self-hosted EKS runners, codified multi-AZ with Terraform, and rightsized recurring workloads across 570+ infra commits.
Result
Sustained monthly AWS near $2,800 — about −30% versus the prior baseline.
