## Rejected — not defects

- **CODE_HEALTH_FRONTEND_A #3** — `container.contains(renderer.domElement)` cleanup guard is correct; `.remove()` is equivalent, not safer.
- **CODE_HEALTH_BACKEND_B #4** — `window`-bound pointer listeners in `FishTankCanvas.tsx` are deliberate: orbit-drag must keep tracking past the canvas edge, and the giant cat's gaze follows an off-canvas cursor. Both are removed in teardown.
- **CODE_HEALTH_BACKEND_B #6** — `targetPos` in `catGiantMesh.ts` is typed `Vec3 | null` under `strict`; can't be `undefined`.
- **CODE_HEALTH_BACKEND_B #1, #2** — logging swallowed `setPointerCapture`/`navigator.vibrate` exceptions would spam the console on routine, expected browser behavior (cancelled pointer id, no user gesture).
- **CODE_HEALTH_FRONTEND_A #5** — dirty-flagging `RigBone.updateMatrices` isn't worth it at ~25 bones/frame; `resetPose()` + layer accumulation dirties nearly every bone every frame anyway.

## Doc gaps report

Directionally correct (class/interface-level TSDoc genuinely missing), but several of its suggested strings were **factually wrong** and were corrected rather than pasted verbatim when adding docs:
- `CatRig` is forward kinematics only, not inverse kinematics.
- `SpringDamper1D`/`3D` damping is a free config parameter, not fixed to critical damping.
- The sub-stepped semi-implicit Euler integration lives in `SpringDamper1D.update`; `SpringDamper3D.update` only delegates to it.
- `GazeTrackingLayer.update` deliberately *adds* a small roll term when turning — it does not "decouple yaw and pitch to prevent rolling."
